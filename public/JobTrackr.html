JobTrackr

Track every job you apply to — automatically, in a Google Sheet you own.

JobTrackr is a Chrome extension for job seekers. As you browse job postings, it reads the details off the page and saves them to a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. No copy-pasting, no re-typing, and no third-party service holding your job-search history.

What it does

Detects postings automatically

Reads job title, company, location, link, and salary where available.

Works beyond the big boards

Supports LinkedIn, Indeed, and Glassdoor, plus any career site that publishes standard job-posting data.

Syncs to your own sheet

Creates a spreadsheet in your Google Drive. You own it, and can sort, filter, or export it however you like.

Stays out of the way

Nothing is saved until you confirm it. Edit any entry, or add applications manually.

How it works

  1. Install JobTrackr and connect your Google account.
  2. JobTrackr creates a spreadsheet in your Drive for your applications.
  3. Browse job postings as normal. When one is detected, the extension asks whether you applied.
  4. Confirm, and the job is written straight to your sheet.

Your data stays yours

JobTrackr has no backend server. Your job applications go directly from your browser to your own Google Sheet, authenticated with your own Google account. Nothing is sent to, stored by, or visible to the developer.

The extension requests only the drive.file permission — the narrowest Google Drive access available. It can only see the spreadsheet it creates for you, and nothing else in your Drive. You can disconnect your account at any time from the extension's settings.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

Install

JobTrackr is available on the Chrome Web Store. It requires Google Chrome and a Google account.
